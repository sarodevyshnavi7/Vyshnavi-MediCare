Our purpose

Vyshnavi MediCare is an educational pharmacy management demonstration designed to make medicine catalogue browsing, inventory visibility and order tracking easier to understand.

What you can do

Explore sample catalogue entries, manage a demo cart, place simulated orders and review order status. Administrators can manage catalogue and stock information.

This is a student demonstration project, not a real pharmacy. Product listings are sample data and do not constitute medical advice.
